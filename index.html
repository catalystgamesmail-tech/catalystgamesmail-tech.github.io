Story Video Bot

Story Video Bot is a personal Windows application operated by catalystgamesmail-tech for their own YouTube channel.

It creates original fictional stories using an installed language model, reads them aloud with text-to-speech, and combines the narration with captions and background footage on the operator's computer.

How it works

  1. Create a video locally, including its narration, captions, title, and description.
  2. Connect the intended YouTube channel and review a private test upload.
  3. Choose whether to enable recurring uploads with future publication times.

Why it asks for Google access

Google authorization lets the application identify the connected YouTube channel and upload videos with their titles, descriptions, audience settings, disclosure settings, and optional publishing times. Uploads start as private. Scheduled videos become public at their configured release times.

Read the privacy policy for details about permissions, local information, and disconnecting the application. The usage terms explain the operator's responsibilities.

About this website

This website provides information about the application. It does not run the application, provide a hosted uploading service, or collect Google sign-in credentials.
